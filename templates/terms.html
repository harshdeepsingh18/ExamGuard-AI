PLATFORM TERMS

Using ExamGuard AI

Account responsibilities

Use an account assigned to you, protect your sign-in details, and provide accurate registration information. Teacher tools remain unavailable until an administrator approves the account.

Exam participation

Follow the exam rules set by the teacher. Answers are saved during an attempt, and submitting the attempt ends your ability to change answers. Objective answers may be scored automatically; written answers require teacher review.

Monitoring

Exam monitoring can record tab visibility, fullscreen changes, and—when you explicitly enable it—face-count results from temporary camera frames. Monitoring signals can be inaccurate or have ordinary causes. They are context for human review and must not be treated as conclusive evidence of misconduct.

Availability and support

The platform depends on the institution's Supabase configuration, network connection, and deployed application. Ask your ExamGuard administrator for support, account changes, and institution-specific policies.
