PRIVACY OVERVIEW

Your data in ExamGuard

Information used

ExamGuard stores account details such as your name and email, exam schedules, submitted answers, results, and notifications in the connected Supabase project.

Teachers may upload PDF question papers. Files are kept in a private storage bucket; extracted text is stored with the exam so the teacher can review it.

Optional camera checks

Camera access starts only after you choose Enable Camera Check and grant browser permission. Small temporary image frames are sent to the ExamGuard server for face counting with MediaPipe and OpenCV. Frames are processed in memory and are not saved as images or video. Face counts and selected monitoring events may be stored with the exam attempt for teacher review.

Monitoring limits

Tab visibility and fullscreen exit events are recorded during an active exam. Events can have ordinary causes and do not establish misconduct on their own. Teachers are responsible for reviewing them with context.

Access and questions

Supabase row-level security limits access to records according to account role and exam ownership. For information about your institution's data retention, account deletion, or privacy contact, contact your ExamGuard administrator.
